About Us

Two Hundred Years
of Tradition

Our company was established in the late 18th century with the takeover of an old oil workshop. The Denizer olive oil factory, founded by Hocazade Abdurrahim Bey and his brother-in-law Salih Reiszade Cevdet Bey, has evolved from a traditional facility using steam-powered machines into a modern enterprise producing with the latest technology.

After a fire in 1959, the facility was rebuilt faithfully to its original architecture and operations continued without interruption. The production building has been classified as a historical heritage site to be passed on to future generations and remains under protection, with production continuing in the same building.

In 1995, production technology was updated to reach a daily processing capacity of 60,000 kg of olives. In 2004, the machinery was renewed to process 120,000 kg per day, placing us among the select facilities in the Edremit Gulf. Our facility, equipped with an Amenduni continuous system, is located in the city center of Havran, Balıkesir province, with a 3,000 m² area, approximately 1,000 m² of enclosed space, and 200 tons of olive oil storage capacity.

Our Vision

A Sought-After Brand Worldwide

To make Edremit Gulf olive oil a particularly sought-after and preferred brand both domestically and internationally. Our facility is located in Havran, in the Edremit Gulf. What differentiates our olive oil from others is the unique microclimate of the Edremit Gulf. Our gulf has the highest oxygen concentration in breathable air after the Alps.

To our north lies the Kazdağları (Mount Ida), historically known as "Mount Ida of a Thousand Springs," a National Park home to endemic species and 75% of Turkey's plant flora and fauna. Throughout history, its perfect geography and abundance of water resources have hosted many ancient city-states.

In these fertile lands where Zeus, king of the gods, lived and where the world's first beauty contest was held, we wish to share what we believe to be our high-quality olive oil — produced from 10 million Edremit Yağlık olive trees — with conscious consumers worldwide. To this end, our facility aims to present Edremit Gulf olive oil to consumers who value and appreciate olive oil, in a historically grounded, consistent, and uncompromising manner.

Our Mission

Preserving Tradition

To preserve the traditional taste of Edremit Gulf olive oil and ensure its continuity, to take action for this purpose, and to implement all necessary measures in the long term. What differentiates our olive oil from other regions is the unique microclimate of the Edremit Gulf.

Our facility has sponsored and participated in every panel in the gulf to raise environmental awareness among producers against any adversity that could cause changes or damage to this microclimate. We take part in every platform to ensure that our core values are preserved and not forgotten.

Cooperating with district agricultural directorates to improve quality from olive harvest through oil production, and playing an active role in civil society organizations, our facility has participated in many fairs and festivals for the promotion of Edremit Gulf olive oil. We also conduct joint projects with the Edremit Trade Exchange for the elimination of environmentally harmful waste from olive oil production.
